## EN-CBT-2 · WHAT IT IS

**Canonical positioning phrase:**

> Pira Labs acts before: when the company still has clients, cash, and reputation, but the working model has started costing too much.

**Definition of the three terms:**

> **"Creative"** because the problem in service businesses is rarely only financial or operational. It is about how value is created, delivered, and perceived. It requires reading the whole system, not just analyzing indicators.
>
> **"Business"** because the focus is the business as a system. Not the product, not the channel, not the tool. The entire business, with its interdependencies, its bottlenecks, and its unrealized potential.
>
> **"Turnaround"** because it is a real transformation with verifiable results. Not a report filed away. Not a plan that assumes the client will implement alone. A turnaround. The business was one way. It became another. The difference is measurable.

## EN-CBT-2B · TRADITIONAL TURNAROUND VS CREATIVE BUSINESS TURNAROUND

> The central difference is timing: traditional turnaround enters in crisis, when options are already reduced. Creative Business Turnaround enters before, when the company still has the conditions to act with clarity.

| Dimension | Traditional turnaround | Creative Business Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| **When it enters** | In crisis: when the fire has already started | Before: when the signals appear |
| **Context** | Company with compromised cash | Company with clients, cash, and reputation |
| **Objective** | Stabilize and survive | Redesign and advance |
| **Focus** | Cost-cutting and financial restructuring | Work, value, technology, and governance |
| **Cost of inaction** | Already high: options reduced | Low now, high if delayed |
| **Available options** | Limited by urgency | Broad: window still open |

## EN-CBT-3 · FOR WHICH COMPANIES

> Creative Business Turnaround serves service businesses that have reached a point where growth has stalled, margin has shrunk, or the founder has become a bottleneck, but the real crisis has not yet arrived.

**Sectors with highest application:**
> Legal, communications and marketing, specialized consulting, and corporate education. Natural expansion: healthcare, HR, engineering, architecture, and other knowledge-intensive professional services.

**The decision-maker profile:**
> A partner, CEO, or president with real autonomy to change. Someone who recognizes the problem, has genuine openness to rethink the model, and is not looking for validation of a decision already made.

## EN-CBT-4 · THE SIGNALS

> Five signals that appear before the crisis. The more present, the closer the right moment to act.

**Signal 1 · Growth without structure**
> The company grew faster than its working design. The team expanded, revenue increased, but the operation became dependent on key people and constant improvisation.

**Signal 2 · Disappearing margin**
> Revenue continues, but results do not follow. Invisible costs, unbilled scope, hours invested beyond contract, pricing misaligned with actual cost.

**Signal 3 · Founder as bottleneck**
> Every important decision passes through the founder. The team waits, delays, cannot move without approval. Not a leadership failure. An absence of clear delegation criteria.

**Signal 4 · Technology that did not convert**
> The company invested in AI. The result was accelerating the noise that already existed. Without method first, technology scales the problem.

**Signal 5 · Value delivered but not perceived**
> The work is good, but the client does not see what they are paying for. Renewal conversations are always difficult. The company feels it is worth more than it can charge.

## EN-CBT-5 · WHAT CREATIVE BUSINESS TURNAROUND IS NOT

> The category exists to name something that did not fit existing categories. Understanding what it is not helps understand what it is.

> CBT does not overlap with strategic consulting, creative agencies, mentoring, or crisis intervention, because none of those solve the business as a system before the problem becomes urgent.

| Category | Why it is not the same |
|---|---|
| **Strategic consulting** | Delivers a report and exits. CBT connects diagnosis to an executable route and, when contracted for execution, stays until results are verified. |
| **Creative agency** | Solves communication or channel. CBT solves the business as a system. |
| **Technology or AI company** | Sells tools. CBT has no proprietary technology product. |
| **Mentoring or coaching** | Works with the decision-maker individually. CBT works with the business as a system. |
| **Crisis intervention** | Arrives after the rupture. CBT acts before, when options still exist. |

## EN-CBT-6 · HOW IT WORKS

**INSPIRA · Diagnosis**
> INSPIRA reads six layers in sequence: **Sopro**, where the company starts and what it declares it wants; **Fôlego**, how it appears to the market and to artificial intelligence engines; **Pulso**, how people and real roles function in practice; **Acústica**, how governance and processes actually operate; **Pressão**, where economic tension is concentrated; and **Suspiro**, what the six layers reveal when read together.
>
> When the six layers are integrated and approved, **Propulsão** enters: the route forward. What changes, who changes, in what order, and how you know it is done. Gabriela Aguiar leads.
